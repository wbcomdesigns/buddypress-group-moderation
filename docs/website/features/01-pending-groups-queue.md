# Pending Groups Queue

**Included in Free**

Go to **Groups > Pending Groups** in your WordPress admin. The menu item shows how many groups are waiting.

The queue lists 20 groups per page, newest first, with the group name, its creator, when it was created and the type the creator asked for. The settings page has a **Pending Groups** button with the same count.

## Approve a group

Click **Approve** in the group's row. The group gets the type its creator asked for (public, private or hidden), the row disappears and the creator gets an on-site notification and an email.

To approve several groups at once, tick them and click **Approve selected**. Clicking it with nothing ticked shows "Tick at least one group to approve."

## Reject a group

1. Click **Reject**. A small form opens inside the row.
2. Optionally type a reason for the creator (up to 500 characters).
3. Click **Reject and delete group**, or **Cancel** to close the form. Pressing Escape also closes it.

Rejecting deletes the group. This cannot be undone. The creator gets an on-site notification ("Your group ... was not approved by the site administrators.") and, when emails are on, an email that includes your reason.

## When two administrators act at once

If another administrator already approved or rejected a group, your click shows "This group was already approved or rejected." and the row is removed.
