# FAQ

**Included in Free**

## Does approval change the group's type?

No. The group gets the type its creator asked for: public, private or hidden.

## Can the creator make the group public before approval?

No. Changing the privacy in Manage > Settings only changes the type that will be applied on approval.

## Can invited members join a pending group?

No. Their invitation is held and appears once the group is approved.

## Can I undo a rejection?

No. Rejecting deletes the group. The creator can create it again.

## Does it work with BuddyBoss?

No. The plugin works with BuddyPress only.

## Does it work with Youzify?

Yes. The pending notice shows on Youzify group pages too.
